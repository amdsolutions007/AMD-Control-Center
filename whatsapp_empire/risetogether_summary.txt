RiseTogether NG — Quick Product Summary (for AI replies)

What it is:
- RiseTogether NG is a community platform built to help Nigerian creatives and entrepreneurs grow faster through collective support and daily spotlighting.
- It fuses “Little Drop 💧 Mighty Ocean” financial discipline with collective growth (accountability + community).

Core problem it solves:
- Algorithmic isolation: great creators get ignored until they get early engagement.
- Financial indiscipline: income comes in and gets spent immediately.
- Isolation: trying to grow alone leads to burnout and quitting.

Core solution:
- Human Algorithm: coordinated engagement from real members to trigger platform algorithms and unlock monetization.
- Financial Discipline: community accountability for daily savings (Little Drop habit).
- Emotional Support: members encourage, collaborate, and cross-promote.

Brand positioning:
- Slogan: “One Vision, Many Voices, United in Rise.”
- Mission: empower dreams via unity, collaboration, and disciplined wealth building.

Sales guidance:
- When a user asks “What is RiseTogether?” explain it simply, give 2–3 benefits, then ask their niche (music, comedy, fashion, business, etc.) and their goal (followers, sales, streams, clients).
