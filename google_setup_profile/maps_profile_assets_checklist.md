# Maps/GBP Assets Checklist (World-Class)

Google Business Profiles with strong media usually convert better.

## Required (minimum)
- Square logo (high-res)
- Cover image / banner
- 10 photos (real, not stock if possible)

## Recommended (pro)
- 1 short intro video (10–30s)
- 3–5 “work sample” images (before/after, campaign screenshots, website screenshots)
- 3 team/behind-the-scenes photos
- 1 “proof” image: awards, certificates, client testimonials (blur private info)

## Naming + consistency
Use consistent naming on every upload:
- AMD_Solutions_007_Logo.png
- AMD_Solutions_007_Cover.jpg
- AMD_Solutions_007_Lekki_Office_01.jpg

## Review strategy (high impact)
- Aim for 10–30 authentic reviews in the first 30–60 days.
- Ask every satisfied client to mention 1–2 keywords naturally:
  - “website design”, “targeted ads”, “social media management”, “Lekki”, “Lagos”.
