Human traders are hardwired with cognitive biases that sabotage profitability:

1. Loss Aversion Bias → Holding losing positions too long (hoping for recovery)
2. Recency Bias → Overweighting recent market moves in decision-making
3. Confirmation Bias → Seeking information that confirms existing positions
4. FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) → Entering trades at peaks
5. Panic Response → Exiting positions at bottoms

These aren't character flaws. They're evolutionary survival mechanisms that worked for hunter-gatherers but destroy trading accounts.
