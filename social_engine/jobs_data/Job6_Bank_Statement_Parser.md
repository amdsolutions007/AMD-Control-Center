## 🛠️ THE MANUAL (TECHNICAL CAPABILITIES)
* **Department:** FinTech & Credit Analysis
* **Clearance:** Level 4 (Financial Data)

### THE BRAIN OF THE PARSER:
1.  **👁️ THE EXTRACTOR (AI OCR):**
    * *Function:* Reads PDFs, images, or scanned statements from any Nigerian bank. Ignores watermarks and blurry text.
2.  **🧠 THE ANALYST (CATEGORIZATION):**
    * *Function:* Separates "Salary/Revenue" from "Gambling/Expenses." Identifies recurring income vs. one-off deposits.
3.  **⚖️ THE JUDGE (SCORING):**
    * *Function:* Auto-calculates Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratio and gives a final "Creditworthiness Score" instantly.
