## WORLD‑CLASS HYBRID ADDENDUM (AI RECOMMENDATIONS)

### 1) Positioning + Offer Clarity
- **Primary promise:** “Instant primary-care triage + safe next steps” (not “full diagnosis”). This improves trust and reduces medical-claims risk.
- **Offer ladder:** Free symptom triage → paid doctor consult (if available) → pharmacy/clinic referral → follow-up check-in.

### 2) Compliance-Safe Wording (Nigeria-first)
- Replace absolute claims like “diagnoses you instantly” with: “**instant symptom triage**,” “**probable causes**,” “**risk level**,” “**what to do next**.”
