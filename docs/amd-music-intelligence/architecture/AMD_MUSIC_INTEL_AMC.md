## 2. Purpose

The Architecture Master Charter (AMC) is the **Constitution of AMD Music Intelligence**.

It establishes the permanent principles, identity, laws, and governance framework under which the platform is conceived, built, operated, and evolved. The AMC defines **what the platform is**, **what it stands for**, and **what must never be violated** — without prescribing how any capability is implemented.

Every future architecture decision, implementation decision, AI capability, business feature, documentation update, and operational procedure must remain consistent with this document.

When conflict arises between this charter and any other document, policy, or informal practice, **this charter prevails** unless formally amended through the Change Management Policy defined herein.

---

## 3. Scope

This charter governs:

- The AMD Music Intelligence platform in its entirety
- All Client Hubs, artists, playlists, and tenant representations hosted on the platform
- All intelligence, discovery, streaming, analytics, and audience capabilities
- All AI-assisted services operating within or on behalf of the platform
- All documentation, governance, and change processes pertaining to the platform

This charter does **not** govern:

- Implementation specifications (reserved for downstream documents)
- Parent-company operations outside the Music Intelligence domain
- Third-party platform policies (DSPs, social networks, cloud providers)

## 7. Platform Identity

AMD Music Intelligence is:

### An AI-Powered Music Intelligence Platform

Not a link aggregator. Not a single-artist page. Not a campaign microsite. AMD Music Intelligence is a **sovereign intelligence platform** that understands music ecosystems, audience behavior, and distribution dynamics — and acts on that understanding through AI-assisted discovery, curation, analytics, and routing.

### A Flagship Intelligence Platform Within AMD Solutions 007

AMD Music Intelligence is the **music intelligence vertical** of the AMD Solutions 007 ecosystem. It inherits the parent brand's standards of executive authority, premium presentation, and operational discipline while maintaining its own constitutional identity and documentation hierarchy.

### Designed for Multiple Stakeholder Classes

| Stakeholder | Platform Relationship |
|---|---|
| **Artists** | Receive discovery exposure, audience intelligence, and distribution reach through Client Hubs |
| **Labels & Collectives** | Operate Client Hubs — branded tenant environments housing artists, playlists, and campaigns |
| **Listeners** | Discover music, engage with AI-assisted curation, and access streaming destinations |
| **Partners** | Integrate with the platform's open architecture for distribution, data, and co-marketing |
| **Enterprises** | Deploy multi-tenant music intelligence at scale with governed analytics and audience ownership |

### A Master Platform — Not a Single Client

The platform natively supports **multiple Client Hubs, artists, and campaigns** under one unified intelligence architecture. No single client, artist, or playlist defines the platform's identity.

## 8. Constitutional Principles

These principles guide every decision. They are not optional preferences.

### 8.1 Intelligence before Automation

Automation exists to amplify intelligence, not to bypass it. Systems must understand context before acting. AI-assisted workflows must be explainable and auditable.

### 8.2 Data before Assumption

Decisions, recommendations, and displayed metrics must derive from recorded, verifiable data. Assumption-driven features are prohibited in production intelligence surfaces.

### 8.3 One Platform. Every Streaming Service.

AMD Music Intelligence is the single sovereign entry point. Users reach Spotify, Apple Music, Audiomack, Boomplay, SoundCloud, YouTube Music, YouTube, and future DSPs **through** the platform — not around it.

### 8.4 AI-Assisted Decision Making

Artificial intelligence augments human judgment for discovery, curation, and analytics. AI does not autonomously override governance, privacy, or brand standards without defined authorization.

### 8.5 Open Architecture

The platform is designed for integration — with streaming services, social discovery channels, partner systems, and future AI capabilities — through defined boundaries rather than closed silos.

### 8.6 Scalable by Design

Every architectural commitment must pass the test: *Does this scale to multiple clients, artists, and markets without reinvention?*

### 8.7 User-First Experience

Premium presentation, responsive design, and intentional interaction patterns serve the user. Visual identity and interaction hierarchy are institutional assets, not disposable styling.

### 8.8 Analytics-Driven Improvement

Platform evolution is informed by measured behavior — click telemetry, audience conversion, engagement patterns — not by opinion alone.

### 8.9 Security and Privacy by Design

Multi-tenant isolation, data protection, and privacy-respecting audience capture are architectural requirements from inception — not retrofitted compliance measures.

## 9. Architectural Laws

These laws are **permanent**. They may only be amended through constitutional change procedures defined in Section 12. Violation of an architectural law requires executive review before any production deployment.

### Law 1 — The Motherboard Is the Visual Identity

The motherboard layout — central intelligence hub with platform routing on defined axes — is the canonical visual identity of AMD Music Intelligence user-facing surfaces. Redesign of this identity requires a formally approved design phase, not incremental drift.

### Law 2 — Streaming and Discovery Are Separate Responsibilities

**Streaming** routes users to external DSP destinations where full playback occurs. **Discovery** builds brand, audience, and engagement through social channels, content, and platform intelligence. These responsibilities must never be conflated in user experience or system design.

### Law 3 — Listen Now Is Streaming-Only

The primary conversion action — Listen Now — opens the Streaming Gateway for DSP selection. It does not route to social profiles, marketing pages, or non-streaming destinations.

### Law 4 — Social Discovery Complements, Never Replaces, Streaming

Social discovery platforms (e.g., TikTok, Instagram) are audience-building channels that drive traffic **into** the platform ecosystem. They are not streaming destinations and must not be presented as equivalent to DSP routing.

### Law 5 — Agent 007 Serves as the Platform Intelligence Layer

Agent 007 is the designated AI intelligence layer of AMD Music Intelligence — responsible for contextual understanding, personalized discovery, and AI-assisted operations within governed boundaries. Agent 007 is not a generic chatbot bolt-on.

### Law 6 — AMD Solutions 007 Remains the Parent Ecosystem

AMD Music Intelligence operates within and reports to the AMD Solutions 007 brand, governance, and strategic direction. Platform identity is distinct but not independent from the parent ecosystem.

### Law 7 — Audience Ownership Is Strategic Capital

Contact capture (email, messaging platforms, community membership) is a first-class platform capability — not an optional marketing afterthought. Audience data belongs to the platform's clients under governed access rules.

### Law 8 — Analytics Must Never Lie

All analytics surfaces — Artist Intelligence, Client Hub dashboards, campaign reports — must display only verifiable, source-derived metrics. Fabricated, estimated-as-factual, or inflated numbers are prohibited.

### Law 9 — Certified Records Are Immutable

Phase-certified production records and locked implementation documents are historical truth. They are referenced, not rewritten. Corrections require new records, not retroactive edits.

### Law 10 — Translation Over Redesign

When extending the platform, existing certified surfaces are **translated** to new requirements — not redesigned without authorization. Preservation of production-certified architecture is the default posture.

## 10. Governance Model

### 10.1 Executive Governance

Executive governance sets strategic direction, approves constitutional changes, resolves cross-domain conflicts, and holds final authority over platform identity and brand standards.

**Authority:** Solutions 007 — Executive Leadership

### 10.2 Technical Governance

Technical governance ensures architectural consistency, reviews implementation against this charter, certifies production releases, and maintains the integrity of multi-tenant infrastructure.

**Authority:** Chief Product Architect · Platform Engineering · Lead Frontend Architect (as applicable per domain)

### 10.3 Documentation Governance

Documentation governance maintains the Enterprise Documentation Suite, enforces the Documentation Integration Protocol, catalogs all documents in the Master Documentation Ledger, and prevents unauthorized modification of locked records.

**Authority:** Documentation Governance as defined in [MDL](../governance/AMD_MUSIC_INTEL_MDL.md) and [DIP](../governance/AMD_MUSIC_INTEL_DIP.md)

### 10.4 AI Governance

AI governance defines acceptable AI capabilities, data usage boundaries, model behavior standards, and human oversight requirements for Agent 007 and all AI-assisted platform features.

**Authority:** Chief Product Architect · AI Governance Board (as constituted)

## 12. Change Management Policy

### 12.1 Constitutional Changes

Changes to Sections 4–9 of this document (Vision, Mission, Values, Identity, Principles, and Architectural Laws) are **constitutional changes**.

- Require written proposal with rationale and impact assessment
- Require executive approval from Solutions 007
- Require version increment (major)
- Require amendment entry with date and approver
- Existing certified production records remain untouched

### 12.2 Document Revisions

Revisions to non-constitutional sections (Governance, Documentation Hierarchy, Change Policy, Glossary) are **standard revisions**.

- Require review by Documentation Governance
- Require version increment (minor for material changes, patch for clarifications)
- Must not contradict architectural laws without constitutional change

### 12.3 Versioning

| Version Component | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Major** | Constitutional change or fundamental governance restructuring |
| **Minor** | Material addition or revision to non-constitutional sections |
| **Patch** | Clarification, typo correction, or non-material update |

### 12.4 Approval Requirements

| Change Type | Approval Required |
|---|---|
| Constitutional amendment | Executive (Solutions 007) |
| New architectural law | Executive + Technical Governance |
| Enterprise Suite document population | Technical Governance + Documentation Governance |
| Locked record correction | New supplemental record — no retroactive edit |
| AI policy change | AI Governance + Executive notification |

## 14. Long-Term Commitments

AMD Music Intelligence commits to:

1. **Maintaining platform integrity** — preserving certified architecture, visual identity, and multi-tenant boundaries across all phases of growth.

2. **Preserving user trust** — delivering truthful analytics, transparent AI behavior, and privacy-respecting audience practices.

3. **Supporting multiple streaming ecosystems** — remaining DSP-agnostic and routing users to the platforms of their choice.

4. **Encouraging artist growth** — providing discovery exposure, intelligence tools, and audience ownership capabilities that amplify artist careers.

5. **Leveraging AI responsibly** — deploying Agent 007 and AI-assisted features with governance, explainability, and human oversight.

6. **Remaining scalable and extensible** — architecting for N Client Hubs, N artists, N campaigns, and N markets without platform reinvention.

7. **Honoring institutional memory** — maintaining immutable production records and append-only decision logs as permanent organizational knowledge.

8. **Evolving without erasure** — extending the platform through translation and additive documentation, not through silent redesign of certified surfaces.
