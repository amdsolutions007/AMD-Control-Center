## 🎓 KEY DECISIONS

### **Intelligence Corrections (2026-02-11)**
- ✅ Nigeria has 36 states (not 37)
- ✅ Reality: 64 Leke Leke members, 24 followers (no 127-member WhatsApp)
- ✅ Leke Leke DOES NOT render RSS images (browser automation required)

### **Strategic Choice: Option B**
- **CEO Rationale:** "Quality Assurance over reckless speed"
- **Result:** Human-in-the-loop via Telegram (reduces ban risk 80%+)
- **Benefit:** Mobile control (CEO approves from anywhere)

### **Technical Decisions**
- **File-Based Trigger:** Simple, reliable, no database needed
- **PIL Templates:** Fast, no API dependencies (future: Gemini image gen)
- **Railway Deployment:** 2 services (Telegram + Ghost Writer)
